# QuickJS WASM artifacts

`_guest.wasm` is a generated artifact extracted from the `quickjs-rs` 0.2.5
universal wheel, SHA-256
`e82240af1f1dd1b2e12bcf169a22a8e0e451e356f0688f2fc3bba886d9b2bb20`.
It is generated by `go generate ./internal/quickjswasm` and originates from
[`langchain-ai/quickjs-rs`](https://github.com/langchain-ai/quickjs-rs) revision
`278cf32d17b07a9ba2951ebc826256eef703182d`, licensed under MIT.

`_transform.wasm` is built by the same generator from the source-controlled
fork under `transform/`. The fork retains the pinned quickjs-rs OXC transforms
and adds a workflow-module transform that preserves the real exported `meta`
binding while compiling the remaining top-level statements into an exported
execution Promise. Its Rust toolchain and dependency graph are pinned by the
crate's `rust-toolchain.toml` and `Cargo.lock`. This is the runtime boundary that supports top-level
`await` and `return` without parsing JavaScript in Go.

Extracted artifact SHA-256 values:

- `_guest.wasm`: `e8dec0ddb980454a0abc4cb54cfe6960a022efdc382a11501fd53f3836862490`
- `_transform.wasm`: `c4781c9556ec79e8af2295e72d56214fa977d04c907d8f50192f7b2c71a0022d`

`_guest_tracked.wasm` is generated from `_guest.wasm` by the Go stores-only
passive dirty-tracking port of [`semistrict/wafl`](https://github.com/semistrict/wafl)
revision `c1585f4c3efbf2ba9354d1989cee8f075d013f27`. The transform inserts a
4 KiB page write barrier and covers scalar stores plus bulk-memory writes.
Its SHA-256 is
`3419fa56544d7dcebf9708a139c2f46d019f595bd675aa04a7a82b35248cac1d`.
